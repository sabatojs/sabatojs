Model Equations and Boundary Conditions

The Boussinesq equations in a square x-z domain are nondimensionalized with the velocity scaled by the free-fall speed, \(U=\sqrt{gH\alpha\Delta T}\) \[ \frac{\partial \zeta}{\partial t} + J(\psi,\zeta) = \frac{\partial T}{\partial x} + \sqrt{\frac{Pr}{Ra}}\,\nabla^2\zeta, \] \[ \frac{\partial T}{\partial t} + J(\psi,T) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{RaPr}}\nabla^2 T, \] \[ \zeta = \nabla^2 \psi. \] \[Ra = \frac{g\alpha\Delta TL^3}{\nu\kappa}\qquad Pr = \frac{\nu}{\kappa}\] The Laplacian is \(\nabla^2=\frac{\partial^2}{\partial x^2}+\frac{\partial^2}{\partial z^2}\). The boundary conditions are no material or heat flow, or \[\psi=0\qquad z=0,H \text{ and } x=0,H\] \[\frac{\partial T}{\partial x}=0 \qquad x=0,H\] \[\frac{\partial T}{\partial z}=0 \qquad z=0,H\]

Numerical Methods

Operator Splitting

Vorticity and temperature are stepped forward separately the advection and diffusion terms. Diffusion terms are calculated using the Crank-Nicholson, implicit in time method. Vorticity and temperature advection are calculated using the Arakawa Jacobian. A leap-frog in time scheme is used, including a Robert filter to control the leap-frog computational mode. The buoyancy term is calculated using a second order finite difference and is included with the Jacobian term in the leapfrog time stepping. Once the vorticity is updated, it is inverted for the stream function by FFT/IFFT.
