Model Equations and Boundary Conditions

Dimensional

\[ \frac{\partial \zeta}{\partial t} + J(\psi,\zeta) = g\,\alpha\,\frac{\partial T}{\partial x} + \nu\,\nabla^2\zeta, \] \[ \frac{\partial T}{\partial t} + J(\psi,T) = \kappa\nabla^2 T, \] \[ \zeta = \nabla^2 \psi. \]

Non-Dimensional

\[ \frac{\partial \zeta}{\partial t} + J(\psi,\zeta) = \frac{\partial T}{\partial x} + \sqrt{\frac{Pr}{Ra}}\,\nabla^2\zeta, \] \[ \frac{\partial T}{\partial t} + J(\psi,T) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{RaPr}}\nabla^2 T, \] \[ \zeta = \nabla^2 \psi. \] \[Ra = \frac{g\alpha\Delta TL^3}{\nu\kappa}\qquad Pr = \frac{\nu}{\kappa}\]
