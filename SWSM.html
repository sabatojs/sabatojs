Shallow Water Spectral Model

Simulations

Mountain Lee Rossby Wave

Model Equations and Boundary Conditions

Shallow water equations on a rotating sphere: \[\begin{align} \frac{\partial u}{\partial t} +\frac{u}{a\cos\phi}\frac{\partial u}{\partial \lambda} +\frac{v}{a}\frac{\partial u}{\partial \phi} -\left(f+\frac{u\tan\phi}{a}\right)v &= -\frac{g}{a\cos\phi}\frac{\partial h}{\partial \lambda}, \\[6pt] \frac{\partial v}{\partial t} +\frac{u}{a\cos\phi}\frac{\partial v}{\partial \lambda} +\frac{v}{a}\frac{\partial v}{\partial \phi} +\left(f+\frac{u\tan\phi}{a}\right)u &= -\frac{g}{a}\frac{\partial h}{\partial \phi}, \\[6pt] \frac{\partial h}{\partial t} +\frac{1}{a\cos\phi}\frac{\partial (hu)}{\partial\lambda} +\frac{1}{a\cos\phi}\frac{\partial (hv\cos\phi)}{\partial\phi} &= 0 \end{align}\] where \(f=2\Omega \text{sin}\phi\) and \(\lambda\) and \(\phi\) are longitude and latitude, respectively.

Numerical Methods

All fields are written in terms of spherical harmonics. For a field \(q\): \[ q(\lambda,\mu) = \sum_{\ell=0}^{N} \sum_{m=-\ell}^{\ell} q_{\ell m}\, P_{\ell}^{m}(\mu)\, e^{im\lambda} \] with \[ \mu = \sin\phi \] The \(P_l^m\) are the associated Legendre polynomials. A triangular truncation (default T42) is used: \(|m| \le \ell \le N\). The fields are transformed from physical into spectral space using FFT and Gaussian quadrature to find the coefficients \[ q_{\ell m} = \int_{-1}^{1} \int_{0}^{2\pi} q(\lambda,\mu)\, P_\ell^m(\mu)\, e^{-im\lambda} \,d\lambda\,d\mu. \] and each harmonic is stepped forward in time. Non-linear terms are multiplied in physical space before the forward transform. The model decomposes the depth into a mean and deviation, \(h=H+\eta\). The mean component (terms proportional to \(H\)) is stepped forward with an implicit-in-time scheme to control the gravity waves. Once the spectral harmonics are updated, they are transformed back to physical space. A \(4^{\text{th}}\) order hyper-diffusion (\(\propto\nabla^4\)) is used to control small scale noise.
